About Us

Our story.

Chef Massimo — Photo

Chef Massimo Ormani began cooking at age fourteen in his native Florence — first at home, then through formal culinary school, then inside some of the city's most prestigious kitchens.

At Cibreo Ristorante, one of Florence's most acclaimed restaurants and a destination for visitors from around the world, Massimo was hand-selected to represent "The Best of Italy" in the United States.

In Los Angeles, he took over the kitchen at Locanda Veneta — consistently rated one of the top three Italian restaurants in Southern California — where he met his wife and partner, Daniela.

In 2002 they opened Massimo Ristorante in Beverly Hills. It earned a place among Zagat's top 100 Italian restaurants in the U.S. and was named one of Los Angeles Magazine's top 50 restaurants in 2008 — nine award-winning years of white-tablecloth fine dining.

Then they chose something more personal. In December 2017, with their son Alessandro beside them, Massimo and Daniela opened Tuscan Son: the same fine-dining quality, without the formality. A place where the neighborhood eats like family.

I yearned to take my passion and experience and create a casual Italian farm-to-table spot where my friends, family, and local food enthusiasts could enjoy my cooking at an accessible price.Massimo Ormani · Chef & Owner

Our Community

"It's a different vibe — it's more fun," says Daniela. "We get a much younger customer, a lot of young families who are glad to see their kids eating healthy. It's less formal and more relaxed. We live in the neighborhood, and people are excited to bring their friends in."

Massimo shops the Santa Monica and Culver City farmers markets every week, handpicking heirloom tomatoes and seasonal produce for that day's menu. We support local schools, farmers markets, and nonprofits — and everything leaves our kitchen in compostable packaging.
